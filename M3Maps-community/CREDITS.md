# Community maps

These 154 pictures come from the Mandelbulb3D 1.99.37 package that Machina
Infinitum offers as a free download on their public website; they are not in
the official MB3D repository (github.com/thargor6/mb3d), whose maps the
program ships as well. They belong to the maps collection of DarkBeam (Luca
GN) at Fractal Forums, whose terms of use come with the program
(`m3maps terms of use.txt` in the M3Maps folder, as for the maps there):

- free to use in personal and commercial projects, without attribution
  required (except the maps named below);
- not to be sold as they are, unchanged or recoloured, in any form;
- not to be used in works encouraging illegal acts.

DarkBeam did not make the pictures: he gathered freely downloadable ones,
some from the M3Maps collections shared on DeviantArt; he thanks CmdrChaos,
Fractales75, Nic022 and UndeadAcademy and all the people who shared. Maps
3046, 3047 and 3048 are by Kronpano (DeviantArt), who allowed them to be
hosted with the others; please credit him when you use them.
