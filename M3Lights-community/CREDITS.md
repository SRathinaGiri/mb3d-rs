# Community light presets

These 608 light presets (`.m3l`, load them in the Lighting window's quick
load list or with Open) come from the Mandelbulb3D 1.99.37 package that
Machina Infinitum offers as a free download on their public website; they
are not in the official MB3D repository (github.com/thargor6/mb3d).

The files name no author and no terms of use. They were made by members of
the Mandelbulb3D community and shared in that package; credit goes to their
makers and to Machina Infinitum for collecting them. If you made some of
them and want them credited by name or removed, please open an issue at
https://github.com/thr32423/mb3d-rs/issues.
