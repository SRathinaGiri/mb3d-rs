# Maps by Geoffrey N. Barnes

These 61 maps, numbers 10001 to 13015, are by Geoffrey N. Barnes, who made
them and gave them to mb3d-rs in November 2026. They ship with his
permission.

- 11001–11025: seamless colour textures (marble, waves, crystals, mosaics,
  stone);
- the others: patterns, tiles and pictures (mazes, checkers, rings,
  circuits, stripes, a mask).

Changes for the program (no picture content changed): pictures with
transparency are laid onto white, as a picture viewer shows them (13004 and
13007 hold their pattern only in the transparency and were black in the
program); 11028 is 4096 instead of 8192 pixels wide (as three floats a pixel
it took 800 MB of memory); the busy colour textures are stored as JPEG at
quality 95 with full colour detail, the rest as the original JPEG or as
lossless PNG; files named `.png` that were JPEG or BMP pictures have the
right name now. 12009, 12010 and 12011 are plain white, black and orange.
